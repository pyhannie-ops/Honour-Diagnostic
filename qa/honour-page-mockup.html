How Honour™ works

Three live sessions, two short private reflections, and a report for everyone involved.

  1. Before we begin

    Free 30-minute discovery call

    We talk through your context and confirm together that Honour™ is the right step. No pitch, no pressure.

  2. Before Session 1

    Intake and consent

    The sponsor completes an intake form and each participant completes a short individual form, so every session starts with the full picture.

  3. Session 1 · Tuesday

    Reveal

    We surface the patterns, friction and unspoken concerns behind the meeting narratives.

  4. After Session 1

    Private reflection (about 15 minutes)

    An email link to a short questionnaire, due the day before Session 2 at the latest.

  5. Session 2 · Friday

    Align

    The team reviews the anonymised findings together and maps where the misalignment sits.

  6. After Session 2

    Private integration (about 15 minutes)

    A second email link, due the day before Session 3 at the latest.

  7. Session 3 · the following Wednesday

    Direct

    We confirm the picture and share clear recommendations.

  8. Within 5 business days

    Your reports

    The sponsor receives the anonymised Honour™ Report. Every participant receives their own report.

Honour™ may be right for you if

  • You sense misalignment but cannot name the specific issue.
  • Strategy sessions, team building or process changes have not solved it.
  • You are preparing for a significant leadership or structural decision.
  • You are ready to hear honest feedback and act on it.

It may not be right if

  • You already know the exact problem and have a plan. You need execution support.
  • You need results in under two weeks.
  • The team is not ready to engage honestly.
  • You want training or skills development. That can follow, but it is not included.

What Honour™ is not

Not a strategy session, training programme, culture survey, team-building exercise or values workshop. It is a diagnostic: the essential first step before any of those can work. The sponsor continues to lead.

Before you decide

Straight answers to the questions worth asking before you commit.

Will your people be honest, and will it be safe for them to be?

Honour™ is designed so that people can tell the truth safely. Each participant responds privately as well as in the sessions, and what is shared with the group and with you is anonymised. Participants complete a consent form first, and data is handled according to POPIA and GDPR. If your team is not ready to engage honestly, please indicate so during the discovery call.

What will you see, and what will your team see?

You receive an anonymised organisational report, and each participant receives only their own individual report. The Honour™ Report contains findings and recommendations, with no names attached to any finding. Individual reports are delivered separately and are not shared with you. Nobody is singled out.

Will you be in the sessions?

Only if the participants agree to it. You remain the leader throughout. Honour™ gives you a clearer picture to lead from, and it does not replace your judgement.

What does it ask of your team's time?

About five hours per participant in total, spread over two weeks, plus a short intake form beforehand. That is three 90-minute online sessions, held on a Tuesday, a Friday and the following Wednesday, and two private reflections of about 15 minutes each. After Sessions 1 and 2, each participant receives an email link to a reflection, due the day before the next session at the latest. Reports follow within 5 business days of the final session.

What does it cost, and what is included?

From $441 per participant (9 people) to $705 per participant (5 people), with no add-ons or hidden tiers. That one rate includes the three facilitated sessions, the sponsor report, individual participant reports and the Gifts of Clarity. A 50% deposit confirms your booking, and the balance is due on report delivery. Honour™ is not offered for fewer than 5 participants. Prices are in USD, with GBP, EUR and ZAR available.

Will it be uncomfortable?

It can be, at first. Honour™ reveals what is truly present, and that is not always easy to hear. That is also where its value lies. The sessions are structured and facilitated, not open-ended, and the process is paced so that nothing is forced.

What happens after the diagnostic?

You decide what happens next. The report gives you a clear picture and recommendations, and you choose which to act on and when. Honour™ does not include training or implementation, but it is the essential first step before any of those can be effective.

What if your team has more than 9 people?

Another Honour™ Diagnostic can be commissioned for a second group. This also surfaces cross-team dynamics that one large session would miss.

How do you know it is the right fit before you commit?

Book the free 30-minute discovery call. You share your context and we confirm together whether Honour™ is right for your team, its timing and its scope. If it is not, we will tell you honestly.
