Priscilla Hannie

CLARITY. FLOW. IMPACT.

Priscilla Hannie is, at heart, a facilitator. She helps groups find truth, alignment, and clarity, whether they meet face-to-face or online. Her talent for creating space for others influences everything she does, from designing systems and writing documentation to authoring fiction and nonfiction about the human side of work.

She has more than twenty years of experience connecting Operations and IT within the financial industry. She is formally trained in Agile delivery and operational systems, and her approach is shaped by narrative coaching, a technique of active listening that guides all the conversations that she leads. Priscilla moves easily between structure and story, and between systems and the people who use them.

She sees clarity as essential to effective leadership. Priscilla supports clients through change, guiding them toward systems that are clear, humane, and sustainable. Rather than providing answers, she creates space for the right answer to emerge.

Priscilla blends analytical thinking with intuitive design. She listens closely, frames ideas with purpose, and makes complex topics easy to understand. Her approach is steady and thoughtful, always centred on the people at the heart of the work.

Whether she is helping teams align, shaping documentation, or guiding creative projects, Priscilla creates an environment marked by presence, care, and quiet confidence. Her work brings structure and purpose together, making real change possible.
